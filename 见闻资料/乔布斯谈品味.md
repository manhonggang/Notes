### 原话一

> “Ultimately, it comes down to taste. It comes down to trying to expose yourself to the best things that humans have done and then try to bring those things into what you’re doing. Picasso had a saying: good artists copy, great artists steal. And we have always been shameless about stealing great ideas, and I think part of what made the Macintosh great was that the people working on it were musicians and poets and artists and zoologists and historians who also happened to be the best computer scientists in the world.” ([A-Z Quotes](https://www.azquotes.com/quote/1056665?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Steve Jobs quote: Ultimately, it comes down to taste. ..."))

**译文：**  
“归根到底，这就是品味。就是让自己去接触人类曾经做过的最好的东西，然后把那些东西带入你正在做的事情里。毕加索曾说：好艺术家借鉴，伟大艺术家‘偷’。我们一直毫不羞愧地‘偷’伟大的点子。我认为 Macintosh 的伟大之处在于，参与其中的人们既是音乐家、诗人、艺术家、动物学家、历史学家，又恰好是世界上最优秀的计算机科学家。”
